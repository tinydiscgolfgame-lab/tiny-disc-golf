Reward Rolls

In Tiny Disc Golf, when a Player performs a spectacular feat like throwing in an Ace or winning a League or Tournament, then you have the chance to earn a random Reward from the Reward Roll Chart below. As with Success Rolls, a lower roll being better and wins the Player the best benefit!

Special Abilities (SA) Discs

If you were rewarded a SA Disc from your Reward Roll, choose the mold of disc you want and then roll once again to determine the type of Special Ability that disc has. So, if your putting is atrocious, you might want a SA Putter or if your Driving is subpar, you might choose a Driver and then roll to see how that disc has been changed to help your game!

Disc Distance Calculator

The Distance Limit Chart caps the Power × 20 portion of the equation. The Power × 20 value is calculated normally, then limited to the chart maximum for the player's Power and disc type. Glide is then added separately: Glide × 10 for drives off the tee and Glide × 5 for throws from the field. Speed 1–3 = Putter, 4–6 = Midrange, 7–9 = Fairway, and 10–14 = Driver.

Modifiers

Use these modifiers when determining a throw or putt. When a modifier has a Control effect, its clickable option on the 1–100 tab applies that percentage as percentage points to the Final Value. Power and Distance effects are shown for reference.

Special Rolls

Success Roll of 1 — Spectacular Success!If a Success Roll ever comes up as a 1, the disc goes directly into the basket as long as the Player can throw that far. This can be an Ace off the tee or a “Field Ace” from the fairway.
Success Roll of 100 (00) — Complete Failure!The attempt was a complete failure and the result is twice as bad as usual. Find out how far off the throw or putt spit-out was and multiply that result by 2.
